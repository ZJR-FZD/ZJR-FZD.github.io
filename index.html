Jingru Zeng / 曾婧茹

Hi there! I am a final-year undergraduate at the South China University of Technology (SCUT), in the School of Computer Science and Engineering.

I will begin my master's study at the Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen (HITSZ), in Computer Technology, working on the China Telecom Xingchen Large Model project.

My research interests lie in large language models (LLMs), multimodal large language models (MLLMs), and LLM agents.

🌱 Miscellaneous

I don't think academics should take up 100% of one's time. Regular exercise, socializing, and leisure matter too — they keep both body and mind in balance.

I love exploring. Research is one kind of exploration, in the domain of professional knowledge, which keeps sparking my curiosity and deeply fascinates me. At the same time, I also enjoy exploring the real world, which makes my life fuller and more refreshing — and occasionally stepping out of my comfort zone brings a real sense of accomplishment. For example, I like joining all sorts of experience classes to try new skills, discovering interesting corners of the city through citywalks and cityrides, or talking with different and interesting people — exchanges that sometimes spark new ideas, broaden my horizons, and inspire me.

My idol is Fan Zhendong. To me he is a pure athlete who stays extraordinarily focused and hardworking in pursuit of his dream, while remaining humble and refined. What impressed me most was his match against a Japanese player at the 2024 Paris Olympics. Trailing badly and bearing the weight of "defending the country's gate," he held his nerve and turned the match around. That's when I became his fan. I can't forget the determination in his eyes — his spirit has kept inspiring me ever since. I hope one day I too can become someone who shines like him.
