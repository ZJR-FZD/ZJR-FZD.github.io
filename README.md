---

## 📖 About This Site

A clean, minimal personal academic homepage built with pure **HTML + CSS + JavaScript** — no build tools, no frameworks. It features:

- **About** — my bio, research interests, and contact links
- **Publications** — my papers with author highlighting
- **Honors & Awards** — scholarships and recognitions
- **Miscellaneous** — a bit about who I am beyond research

The design is adapted from [TidalHarley/TidalHarley.github.io](https://github.com/TidalHarley/TidalHarley.github.io) — a huge thanks to the original author for the beautiful, elegant template. 🙏
